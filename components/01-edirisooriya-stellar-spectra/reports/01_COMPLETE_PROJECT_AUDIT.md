## 1. Executive Summary

This comprehensive audit independently inspects every file, dataset, model checkpoint, explainability script, evaluation metric, and documentation artifact in the Stellar Spectra Research Module. The objective is to verify scientific and computational validity, detect any methodological flaws (such as data leakage, unsupported label claims, or unverified atomic matches), and provide a rigorous, evidence-based foundation for Progress Presentation 1 (PP1).

### Key Audit Findings:
1. **Dataset Provenance & Real Data:** All FITS files on disk in `raw_spectra/` (1,033 files total, 1,004 indexed in manifest, 1,000 processed) are genuine SDSS-IV APOGEE DR17 near-infrared ($H$-band: 15,100–17,000 Å) high-resolution ($R \approx 22,500$) combined spectra from the Apache Point Observatory (APO 2.5m) and Las Campanas Observatory (LCO 2.5m). Header records confirm APOGEE Reduction Pipeline version `0.17.22`.
2. **Critical Discovery — Split Indexing Bug & Data Leakage in Historical Split:** The historical split generated by `01_data_preprocess.py` had a Python array indexing bug where `val_idx` and `sub_train_idx` from the secondary split were applied directly to the master array rather than the first-stage partition (`train_val_idx`). This produced a subtle, unintentional **102-sample overlap between train and test** (68% leakage into the 150-sample test set), explaining why historical test accuracy reached 91.33%.
3. **Corrective Action & Dual Reporting:** We preserve the historical checkpoints and results (`models/best_model.pth` etc.) for historical auditability and implement a clean, zero-leakage, group-aware stratified split pipeline (`01_data_preprocess_corrected.py` and `models/corrected_split/`). Both sets of results are rigorously documented and compared side-by-side.
4. **Proxy Spectral Labels:** Labels ('K', 'G', 'F', 'A', 'B') are effective-temperature proxy classifications derived from ASPCAP/FPARAM $\text{T}_{\text{eff}}$ measurements in FITS HDU 9/10, *not* independently verified Harvard MK spectral types. The astronomical sample is heavily skewed toward cool giants (K: 64.6%, G: 25.2%), with scarce hot stars (A: 2.5%, B: 1.6%).
5. **SHAP & XAI Validation:** `shap.GradientExplainer` executes cleanly on the PyTorch 1D-CNN-BiLSTM model. However, scientific feature discovery requires objective faithfulness (AOPC) and stability (Jaccard similarity) testing against randomized baselines, which were previously missing and are implemented in this audit.

### 3.1 Dataset & Preprocessing
* **FITS Structure and Provenance:** `VERIFIED COMPLETE`. Real SDSS APOGEE DR17 `apStar` multi-extension FITS. Primary HDU header contains telescope target flags, HDU 1 contains combined logarithmic rest-frame flux arrays, HDU 9/10 contain ASPCAP stellar parameters.
* **Effective Temperature Proxy Mapping:** `VERIFIED COMPLETE`. Boundaries: K (3500–5000 K), G (5000–6000 K), F (6000–7500 K), A (7500–10000 K), B (10000–30000 K). Zero NaN values in active 1,000-star catalog.
* **Wavelength Calibration & Resampling:** `VERIFIED COMPLETE`. Log-linear grid from $\log_{10}(\lambda) = 4.179$ ($15,100.8$ Å) to $4.2304$ ($16,999.8$ Å) resampled onto 4,000 uniform points. Preserves key atomic features with resolution $\approx 0.47$ Å/pixel.
* **Dataset Splitting & Leakage:** `PARTIALLY COMPLETE (BUG IDENTIFIED)`. The historical split suffered from a nested index slice bug causing 102 samples of test data to be seen during training. A corrected, group-aware split is required.

## 5. Identified Technical & Scientific Risks and Action Plan

1. **Risk 1 (Critical): Historical Data Leakage Impact on Published Accuracy.**
   - *Mitigation:* Explicitly document the indexing bug in Report 04. Retrain the 3 models on the verified zero-leakage split in `models/corrected_split/` and report both sets of numbers side-by-side with scientific transparency.
2. **Risk 2 (Scientific): Class Imbalance (K dwarfs/giants constitute 64.6%).**
   - *Mitigation:* Document balanced accuracy and per-class recall. Emphasize that overall accuracy is driven by majority classes, while minority hot classes (B, A) reflect extreme scarcity in the infrared survey.
3. **Risk 3 (XAI Novelty): Attribution Peaks Matching by Coincidence.**
   - *Mitigation:* Implement a rigorous Monte Carlo random baseline in `04_scientific_validation.py`. Compute empirical p-values demonstrating whether true SHAP peaks align with atomic transitions significantly better than random wavelength selections.
4. **Risk 4 (Exam Panel Defense): Describing Proxy Classes as Harvard Types.**
   - *Mitigation:* Ensure all reports, app pages, presentation slides, and Q&A documents strictly specify "Effective-Temperature Proxy Classes".
